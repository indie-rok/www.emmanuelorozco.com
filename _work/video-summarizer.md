## What is it?
[Video Summarizer for YouTube](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/video-summarizer-for-yout/lncboggphchcbieioagnogdnjcaekone) is a Chrome extension. It adds a ✨ button to the YouTube player and a Summary button to Shorts.

One click grabs the video's transcript and opens ChatGPT or Claude with your prompt and the transcript already sent. The summary arrives in your own chat. No API keys, no sign-up and no server.

## My work
- Built the transcript extraction and handoff script that fills in and sends the prompt in ChatGPT or Claude.
- Published it on the Chrome Web Store.
