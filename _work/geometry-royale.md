## What is it?
Geometry Royale is a top-down arena shooter for iPhone and iPad. **Up to 4 players** join the same room with a 4-digit code, nearby over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

It has four modes (practice, endless solo, endless co-op and versus), four shapes to play as, each with its own speed, attack and shield, and seven weapons, from fists to a shotgun.

## My work
- Designed the modes, the shapes and weapons and the look and feel.
- Built one game simulation for every mode and 1 to 4 players. The host runs it and sends the game state to the other players 20 times a second.
