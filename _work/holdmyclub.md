## What is it?
[Hold My Club](https://holdmyclub.com/) is a Paris community of social events built around hobbies, with **600+ members**. Its main product is [Circles](https://holdmyclub.com/circles): **6 people, matched by energy and values, who meet one evening a week for 6 weeks** for activities we organize. It costs €59 for the six weeks.

## My work

I led product design and development, using coding agents to implement and test the platform, while owning product decisions, code review, customer interviews and operations.

### Product
- Designed Circles and its evenings, using member feedback as a guide.
- Built an AI sales CRM: a pipeline board and automated follow-ups with dormant customers.
- Built an internal tool to form and manage each circle.
- Host the evenings and the regular events.

### Marketing
- Grew the [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/holdmyclub_paris/) account to **1.1k followers** (so far).
- Run email campaigns to a list of 900 subscribers.
- Plan and create Meta ads.
- Use AI agents to analyze campaign data and improve the next ones.

### Sales
- Sell every circle myself: intro calls, picking each group, personal payment links and WhatsApp follow-ups, including recovering stalled applications.
- Built the sales funnel, from discovery on Instagram to an intro call on Cal.com to payment, now at **600 € MRR**.
- Built AI agents for outreach:
  - **Venues**: Automatically find Paris venues , score them and contact the owners by email, Instagram and Privateaser.
  - **Facebook groups**: watch 30+ groups, flag relevant posts as leads and schedule posts promoting our events.
  - **Instagram**: track trending topics, competitors and their ad targeting.
