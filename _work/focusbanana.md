## What is it?
[Focus Banana](https://focusbanana.com) helps you focus by working next to someone on video. You book a 25, 50 or 75-minute session with another person, or start one right away with an **AI focus buddy**.

Before a session you write one goal that you work during the length of your session.

It also has a **Chrome extension** that turns every new tab into your weekly goals, today's actions and a focus timer. Every 25 focused minutes grows a tree in your virtual garden.
