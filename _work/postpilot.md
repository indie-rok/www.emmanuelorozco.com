## What is it?
Post Pilot tests your Reddit launch post before real people see it. It reads your codebase to understand the product, builds a community of AI personas from real subreddit data, and simulates the thread: upvotes, downvotes and comments. You get a scorecard and a rewritten post.

It's open source. [Link to repo](https://github.com/indie-rok/postpilot).

## My work
- Designed the flow: scan the codebase, write or generate the post, pick a community, simulate, then read the scorecard and the rewrite.
- Built the simulation on CAMEL-AI's OASIS, a social network simulator for LLM agents.
- Built the scorecard (sentiment, engagement, conversion rate).
