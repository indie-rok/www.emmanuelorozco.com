## What is it?
HelloBot is a help widget for ADEO's internal developer tools. Product teams add it to their product with a JavaScript script tag or an npm dependency, and developers open it from a launcher in the corner of the page. It is now in **7 ADEO products**.

From the launcher, developers get:

- **Guided tour**: a step-by-step walkthrough of how to use the product.
- **News** about the tool they are using.
- **Feedback** that goes straight to the product team.
- **Trainings** on best practices for the product.
- **Documentation, Team and Configuration** shortcuts to ask for or find help.

Product owners manage all of this from a back office. They can toggle the widget's visibility, disable specific sections and check usage analytics.

It cut onboarding time from 20 to 30 minutes (training videos) to 2 to 3 minutes (hands-on, inside the tool).
