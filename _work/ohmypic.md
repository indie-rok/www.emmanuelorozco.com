## What is it?
Oh My Pic turns a walk into a photo hunt. You pick what to hunt (a color like yellow, a shape like round, or the community's daily prompt) and how long to walk, then go out and snap every match you find.

## My work
- Designed the UI: sticker-style cards with ink borders and hard shadows, mint accents and Rubik type.
- Built the app: walk timer and GPS distance, the photo hunt, the collage picker and the postcard export.
- Built the back end for the daily hunt and feed: posts with photo uploads, hearts, reports and moderation, with Apple and Google sign-in.
