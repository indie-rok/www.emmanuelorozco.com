Does the thing
once you've told it what to look for.

Loop

Figures out
what to look for.

The loop doesn't verify itself.

"done"is a claim, not a proof

Two people build the same loop.

One uses it to move faster on work they understand.
The other uses it to avoid understanding the work.

The loop can't tell the difference. You can.
